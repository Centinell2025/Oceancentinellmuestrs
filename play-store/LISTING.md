# OceanCentinell Business

Publisher: Beacon of the Eagle LLC. Free B2B demonstration; enterprise contracts are separate. Category: Business. Default language: en-US. Intended audience: adults. No ads or in-app checkout. Package: com.beaconoftheeagle.oceancentinellbusiness. Version: 1.0.0 (1).

Short description: Explore maritime fleet, incident and maintenance workflows for your business.

Full description:
OceanCentinell Business is a free demonstration of maritime operations workflows for enterprise evaluation. Explore a fictional fleet and equipment inventory, filter vessels by sample port, create synthetic incident records, update incident and maintenance status, and prepare a pilot-scope checklist. Export an executive brief, a pilot brief, CSV registers or local JSON records to a destination you select.

This version uses synthetic sample data and locally entered demonstration records. An optional regional AIS layer requests observations through the OceanCentinell server. AISStream account activation is pending for this release; the map clearly shows whether the provider is connected. No sample vessel is presented as a live vessel. Telemetry, remote network monitoring, automated alerts and live AI are not implemented. Company accounts, multi-user permissions and cloud synchronization are not included. Use synthetic data only. Records are stored locally without an additional application encryption layer. Removing app data or uninstalling can remove them.

Not for navigation, emergencies or safety-critical decisions. B2B services, integrations and enterprise licensing require a separate agreement with Beacon of the Eagle LLC. No purchase, billing or subscription is implemented in this demonstration.
