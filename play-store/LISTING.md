# OceanCentinell Business

Publisher: Beacon of the Eagle LLC. Free B2B demonstration; enterprise contracts are separate. Category: Business. Default language: en-US. Intended audience: adults. No ads or in-app checkout. Package: com.beaconoftheeagle.oceancentinellbusiness. Version: 1.0.0 (1).

Short description: Explore maritime fleet, incident and maintenance workflows for your business.

Full description:
OceanCentinell Business is a free demonstration of maritime operations workflows for enterprise evaluation. Explore a fictional fleet and equipment inventory, filter vessels by sample port, create synthetic incident records, update incident and maintenance status, and prepare a pilot-scope checklist. Export an executive brief, a pilot brief or local JSON records to a destination you select.

This version uses synthetic sample data and locally entered demonstration records. It does not provide live AIS/GPS tracking, telemetry, remote network monitoring, automated alerts or live AI. Company accounts, multi-user permissions and cloud synchronization are not included. Use synthetic data only. Records are stored locally without an additional application encryption layer. Removing app data or uninstalling can remove them.

Not for navigation, emergencies or safety-critical decisions. B2B services, integrations and enterprise licensing require a separate agreement with Beacon of the Eagle LLC. No purchase, billing or subscription is implemented in this demonstration.
